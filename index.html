Hi, I'm

Emily Proulx

Writing & Rhetoric Educator · Texts and Technology PhD Student · University of Central Florida

I believe that to be an effective teacher, you must first be a lifelong learner — evolving with the times and with the students in front of you. For more than a decade at UCF I've taught writing as a social, rhetorical, and deeply human activity, treating technology as a literacy to be studied and questioned rather than a threat to be policed. This portfolio collects my teaching philosophy, course design work, and professional record in one place.
